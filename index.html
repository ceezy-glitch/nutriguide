FOOD → HEALTH

Your diet can change more than the number on the scale.

A healthier eating pattern can influence blood glucose, blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, liver fat, digestion, energy, and joint stress. For many patients, the same core habits improve several of these at once.

THE BIG PICTURE

Build a pattern you can repeat, not a diet you can only tolerate for a week.

Across many conditions, the same fundamentals keep showing up: more plants and fiber, enough protein, mostly unsaturated fats, fewer heavily processed foods, and portions that fit your energy needs.

½ Plants

Vegetables and fruit add fiber, potassium, micronutrients, and volume.

¼ Protein

Fish, poultry, eggs, beans, lentils, tofu, yogurt, or other protein that fits your needs.

¼ High-fiber carbohydrate

Whole grains, beans, lentils, potatoes, or other minimally processed starches.

Mostly unsaturated fats

Olive or canola oil, nuts, seeds, avocado, and fish more often than butter or fatty meats.
