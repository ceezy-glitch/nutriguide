EASY FOOD SWAPS

Change the default, not your whole life.

Small substitutions can lower sodium, added sugar, saturated fat, or calories while adding fiber and nutrients.

Sweet cereal → oatmeal

Choose plain oats and add fruit, cinnamon, nuts, or seeds. This generally adds more fiber and gives you control over added sugar.

Soda → water or unsweetened drinks

A simple way to cut a large source of added sugar and calories without making the rest of the meal smaller.

Chips → unsalted nuts or fruit

Useful when you want a snack with less sodium and more fiber, protein, or unsaturated fat.

Butter → olive or canola oil

Replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fat is more heart-friendly than simply adding healthy foods on top of the same pattern.

Processed meat → beans, fish, or lean protein

This can reduce saturated fat and sodium while making room for more fiber-rich foods.

Dessert every night → fruit most nights

You do not need to ban dessert. Making fruit the default more often can reduce added sugar and increase fiber.

ONE-WEEK STARTER

Seven days. Seven manageable actions.

Do not try to change everything at once. Use this as a starter checklist, then repeat what works.

Day 1Write down your main goal and one number you are following, such as weight, blood pressure, A1C, or LDL.
Day 2Replace one sugary drink with water or an unsweetened option.
Day 3Add one high-fiber food: beans, oats, lentils, vegetables, whole fruit, or whole grains.
Day 4Compare two versions of a food label and choose the option that better fits your goal.
Day 5Build one meal as ½ plants, ¼ protein, and ¼ high-fiber carbohydrate.
Day 6Plan tomorrow’s meals before you get hungry.
Day 7Pick the two changes that felt easiest and make them your defaults next week.
GROCERY GUIDE

What to keep around the house

Convenience matters. Your environment should make the healthier option the easier option.

Produce

Fresh or frozen vegetables, whole fruit, berries, salad greens, onions, peppers.

Protein

Beans, lentils, fish, chicken, eggs, tofu, plain Greek yogurt, lower-sodium options when possible.

High-fiber carbs

Oats, barley, brown rice, whole-grain bread, potatoes, beans, lentils.

Healthy fats

Olive/canola oil, unsalted nuts, seeds, avocado, natural nut butter.

Easy snacks

Fruit, yogurt, vegetables with hummus, unsalted nuts, air-popped popcorn.

Flavor without relying on salt

Garlic, lemon, vinegar, pepper, paprika, cumin, herbs, salt-free spice blends.
